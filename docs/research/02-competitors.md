# Competitor teardown

Most sources here are vendor pages, app-store listings, funding coverage or affiliate reviews. Treat feature details as a snapshot and verify them in each product before quoting.

## The landscape at a glance

| Category | Products | Strength | Gap for Stonk Up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Broker paper trading | Zerodha, Moomoo, TradingView Paper, Webull | Real order types and execution | Open-ended, with no curriculum or decision feedback |
| Simulator competitions | Investopedia Simulator, StockGro, Frontpage, Neostox, MockTrade | Social features, leaderboards, habit loops | Rank by returns, which rewards luck and leverage. Live data and money prizes face regulatory risk in India. |
| Replay and backtesting | TradingView Bar Replay, Forex Tester, FX Replay, CandleDrill | Practice on real history | Built for active traders. No guidance; trades aren't scored or journaled. |
| Blind chart games | ChartGame.com, Ticker League, Bulliq, PaperTrader, Chart Quiz | Fast, fun, hides hindsight | Test only "up or down". No plan, sizing or explanation of why. |
| Options strategy tools | Sensibull, AlgoTest | Strategy builders and backtests | Assume expertise. Focus on F&O, where 93% lose. |
| Literacy apps | Zogo, Finny | Bite-size lessons and rewards | Shallow on markets; no simulation |
| Structured courses | Zerodha Varsity, NSE Paathshala | Depth and trust | Passive reading with no practice-feedback loop |
| School game | SIFMA Stock Market Game | Proven in a randomized trial | Classroom-only |

## Closest competitors in detail

### StockGro (India) — highest overlap, different values
- Social investing app with virtual trading on real-world data, challenges, leaderboards, chat and a learning academy (fundamentals, technical analysis, strategies). [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/stockgro), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/stockgro-trade-with-experts/id1533672206)
- **Monetization:** paid in-app portfolio contests with reward money (one report said 70,000 daily paid-challenge participants), plus advisory through SEBI-registered advisers. [Lapaas](https://voice.lapaas.com/startup/stockgro/business-model/), [KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/indias-stockgro-gamifies-stock-market-investments-startup-stories)
- **Scale and money:** ₹150 Cr Series B and a confidential IPO pre-filing. [Entrepreneur](https://india.entrepreneur.com/en-in/news-and-trends/stockgro-raises-inr-150-cr-in-series-b-round-from-mukul/500319), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/stockgro-confidentially-files-ipo-papers-eyes-up-to-%E2%82%B92500-cr-issue/)
- **Our angle:** they rank by returns and pay cash prizes. That's the behaviour we want to train out, and it's increasingly exposed to SEBI's no-monetary-incentive rule and the Online Gaming Act (see [05](05-regulation-and-data.md)). We position as the **process-first, money-free** alternative.

### Zerodha Varsity (India) — the trust benchmark
- Free. Each module has three difficulty levels, each ending in a quiz. Content is bite-sized cards, with certification after each module (60% needed on every quiz and on the exam). [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/featured/introducing-the-varsity-mobile-app), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/varsity-by-zerodha/id1474610753)
- One secondary source mentions streaks, badges and progress tracking. Users report bugs in certification and quizzes.
- **Our angle:** Varsity explains; we let people **practice**. Possible content-linking partner later, not a direct fight.

### Investopedia Simulator (global)
- Free stock, options and crypto simulator with games, leaderboards and a "learn" tab. Often named the best free simulator for beginners. [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/investing/learn/virtual-trading-stock-market-simulators), [ChartingLens](https://chartinglens.com/blog/best-stock-market-simulator-paper-trading)
- **Our angle:** open-ended free trading with no coaching. Leaderboards reward returns.

### TradingView Bar Replay and Forex Tester
- Replay history bar by bar at adjustable speed. Forex Tester offers 20+ years of tick data. Bar Replay doesn't track replay trades without an external journal. [TradingView docs](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000712747-bar-replay-how-and-why-to-test-a-strategy-in-the-past/), [comparison](https://supa.is/article/forex-tester-vs-tradingview-bar-replay-manual-backtesting-2026)
- **Our angle:** the same replay idea, but beginner-friendly, scripted, with checkpoints and a debrief.

### Blind chart games
- ChartGame.com shows an unnamed S&P 500 chart one day at a time. Ticker League hides the name and asks for one up/down call, with practice and streak modes. Bulliq, PaperTrader and CandleDrill run similar blind replays. [HowTheMarketWorks on ChartGame](https://www.howthemarketworks.com/?p=3635), [Ticker League](https://tickerleague.com/games/stock-chart-direction), [PaperTrader](https://www.producthunt.com/p/paper-trader-2/papertrader-2)
- **Our angle:** blind mode is a good idea that we should borrow, but direction-guessing teaches pattern-gambling. We add plan, size, confidence and debrief.

### Zogo (US) — distribution model to learn from
- 400+ bite-size modules and an in-app currency ("pineapples"). Distributed through credit unions and banks. FINRA's investor-education content went live on Zogo in 2026. [FINRA](https://www.finra.org/media-center/newsreleases/2026/finra-makes-investor-education-content-available-zogo-financial), [Business of Business](https://www.businessofbusiness.com/articles/why-the-23-year-old-co-founder-of-zogo-wants-to-gamify-financial-literacy-for-gen-z-bolun-li/)
- **Our angle:** a B2B2C route (brokers, banks, colleges) and regulator-content partnerships are proven paths.

## Mechanics benchmark: Duolingo
- Growth came mainly from **current-user retention**. Their model showed retention of current users had about 6× the DAU impact of other levers. Streaks and leagues amplified engagement, but only on top of a product people already valued. [Lenny's Newsletter (Jorge Mazal)](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/104096876/the-streak-vector)
- Lesson: make the Lab worth returning to, then tune streaks. Test every mechanic; some didn't move retention.

## Where Stonk Up wins
Guided, scenario-based practice that scores **decision quality and calibration**, uses no real money or live data, and treats beginners as learners rather than as gamblers to monetize.
