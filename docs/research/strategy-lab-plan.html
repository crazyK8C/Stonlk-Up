Stonk Up · Study & Strategy Lab · research plan · Oct 2026

Build calibrated confidence, not lucky streaks.

Most first-time traders don't fail for lack of tips. They fail because nobody trains their decision process before real money is on the line, and because the market's feedback is too noisy to learn from. Below is the evidence, the competitor landscape, the skills worth mastering, and a guided plan for a Study section and a simulation-driven Strategy Lab.

Score the decision, not the P&L Practice on historical + synthetic markets Unlock complexity only after risk discipline
1 · The problem, with evidence

Beginners lose money, and they don't learn from losing.

Regulator data and long-running academic studies point the same way: inexperienced traders overtrade, size positions badly, follow the crowd, and sell winners while keeping losers.

The opening: real markets give beginners loud, random feedback. A simulator can give them clean, structured feedback. On a single trade, outcome and decision quality are mixed together, so people learn the wrong lessons. A lab that scores the plan, the sizing and the confidence before revealing the outcome teaches the part that transfers.
2 · Pain points of traders with no experience

Eight reasons beginners lack confidence, and what the product should do about each.

P1

No safe place to practice

They learn by losing real money, often leveraged (SEBI[1]).

Fix: unlimited simulated practice that costs nothing and feels like it matters.

P2

Feedback is noisy

A good decision can lose and a bad one can win, so live trading teaches little (Brazil study[2]).

Fix: score process and calibration separately from outcome. Show the outcome distribution, not one result.

P3

Overconfidence and overtrading

The most active traders earn the least (Barber & Odean[3]).

Fix: confidence sliders, Brier scores and a calibration curve. Charge a time cost for every extra trade in sims.

P4

Can't size risk

Small accounts take big leveraged bets (SEBI income data[1]).

Fix: position-sizing drills and a risk-of-ruin simulator (try it below).

P5

FOMO and herding

Crowded buys tend to underperform afterwards (Robinhood study[4]).

Fix: behavioral pressure drills with hype spikes, fake "everyone is buying" feeds and a timer.

P6

Selling winners, holding losers

The disposition effect is near-universal (Odean[7]).

Fix: require pre-committed exits in every sim trade and flag deviations in the debrief.

P7

Bad information diet

Young investors rely on finfluencers and miss fraud signals (FINRA[6]).

Fix: "spot the red flag" study modules, plus a claim checker that separates fact, model and opinion.

P8

Panic in volatile markets

Poorly timed exits cost about 1.2% a year on average (Morningstar[5]).

Fix: replay real crashes blind and practice staying with a plan through a drawdown.

3 · Competitor landscape

Lots of places to trade fake money. Almost none that teach the decision.

Competitors split into realistic tools with no teaching and teaching content with no practice. Most simulators rank players by returns, which rewards luck and leverage. That's the habit we want to break.

4 · What beginners should master (beyond picking stocks)

Ten skills, each with a drill the Lab can run.

These become the Study tracks. Each one pairs a short concept lesson with a hands-on simulation drill.

MINDSET

Probabilistic thinking

Thinking in odds and ranges instead of certainties. The foundation for everything else.

Drill: confidence-weighted predictions, scored with a Brier score and shown on a calibration curve.

RISK

Position sizing & ruin

How much to risk per idea, and why a winning strategy can still blow up an account.

Drill: the 100-lives risk-of-ruin simulator below.

MATH

Expected value & costs

Win rate × payoff, minus fees, slippage and taxes. Costs are part of why most F&O traders lose.

Drill: the same strategy, re-run with realistic costs switched on.

PROCESS

Trade planning

Thesis, entry, exit and invalidation, written down before acting.

Drill: a plan card is required before every sim. Deviations are flagged in the debrief.

BEHAVIOR

Loss aversion & disposition

Cutting losers and letting winners run, against instinct.

Drill: replays where the plan says exit and the chart begs you to wait.

BEHAVIOR

FOMO, herding & revenge trading

Noticing social pressure and tilt before they move your hand.

Drill: hype-feed scenarios on a timer, plus a cool-down rule after losses.

INFO

Information hygiene

Sorting fact from model from opinion, and spotting scam red flags.

Drill: find the red flags in a mock finfluencer post.

MACRO

Market regimes

How rates, inflation and recessions change what works.

Drill: run one strategy through 2008, 2020 and 2022, blind.

PORTFOLIO

Diversification & time horizon

Correlation, rebalancing, and why time in the market matters.

Drill: allocate ₹10 lakh of virtual money, then stress-test it with shocks.

RESEARCH

Testing a strategy honestly

Overfitting, survivorship and lookahead bias, and why a backtest isn't a promise.

Drill: spot the flaw in a too-good-to-be-true backtest.

5 · The guided approach

A confidence ladder: earn each rung before the next one opens.

Scaffolding moves from a worked example to faded hints to independent practice. Leverage and complex instruments stay locked until risk discipline has been shown, so the app never fast-tracks people into the segment where most lose.

RUNG 1

Know

Short concept card with a worked example

RUNG 2

Predict

Predict-then-reveal questions with a confidence slider

RUNG 3

Plan

Write a plan card: thesis, entry, exit, size

RUNG 4

Simulate

Guided replay with checkpoints and hints

RUNG 5

Stress

Shocks, hype feeds and drawdowns, with no hints

RUNG 6

Reflect

Debrief comparing process with outcome, plus bias tags and a journal entry

RUNG 7

Graduate

Consistency badge: for example 20 sims within risk rules and calibration error under target

6 · Strategy Lab: how one simulation works

Brief, plan, run, decide, debrief, then try a variant.

01 BRIEF

Scenario card

Context and what you know, with no hindsight. The date and ticker can be hidden.

02 PLAN

Commit

Entry, exit, size and confidence %, locked before the run.

03 RUN

Time-stepped market

Candles play out. Hold, add, trim or exit at checkpoints.

04 PRESSURE

Events fire

Earnings, a rate surprise, hype posts and a timer.

05 DEBRIEF

Score the decision

Process score, plan adherence and calibration, plus what was knowable then.

06 VARIANT

Run 1,000 versions

The same plan on many simulated paths shows the spread of outcomes.

↺ Next scenario adapts to your weakest skill tag (spaced practice).

Historical replays

The 2008 crisis, the 2020 COVID crash and recovery, 2022 rate hikes. Uses historical or delayed data only, playable blind.

Synthetic markets

Generated price paths with chosen trend, volatility and regime switches. Labelled as models.

Shock drills

Rate hike, inflation spike, earnings miss, gap down. Practice the reaction you planned.

Behavior drills

FOMO feed, losing streak, revenge-trade trap. Scored on staying disciplined.

Portfolio stress test

Allocate ₹10 lakh of virtual money, then hit it with scenarios. Track drawdown, volatility and diversification.

7 · A taste of the Lab

Same winning strategy, 100 traders. Only the bet size changes.

Each simulated trader makes 100 trades with a real edge: a 45% win rate, where winners pay 1.5× what losers cost. The only thing that differs is how much of the account they risk per trade. This is an illustrative model, not a forecast.

8 · Build plan on top of the current MVP

Three phases, each shippable on its own.

PHASE A · FOUNDATIONS

Study tracks + calibration

  • Confidence slider on existing questions, with Brier score and a calibration chart in Stats
  • Study tracks built from the 8 levels: concept card, worked example, then a quiz
  • Risk-of-ruin sandbox (this page's simulator) as the first Lab drill
  • Synthetic market generator (seeded, layered models). No data license needed, so it ships first
  • Skill tags on attempts so practice adapts to weak spots
PHASE B · STRATEGY LAB v1

Replays + debriefs

  • Plan card that locks before each run, with a plan-adherence score
  • Debrief screen separating process score from outcome, with bias tags and the earned-win / bad-beat / dumb-luck / deserved-loss grid
  • 3 scripted historical replays (2008, 2020, 2022), only once a data license is signed
  • Blind mode: date and ticker hidden until the debrief
PHASE C · DEPTH

Pressure, portfolios, coach

  • Behavior drills (FOMO feed, losing streaks) and a decision journal
  • Portfolio stress tests with ₹10 lakh of virtual money
  • Grounded AI coach that explains debriefs from vetted content only
  • Cohort challenges ranked by process score, never P&L

Guardrails
No live or recent pricesSEBI restricts real-time data sharing. Its 2026 education circular reportedly requires at least a 30-day lag. Use synthetic, macro, or licensed historical data[16][17].
No money in the reward loopSEBI's education rule reportedly bars monetary incentives, and the Online Gaming Act 2025 bans real-money games. Coins never convert to cash, and there are no paid contests[18].
18+ at launchIndia's DPDP rules treat under-18s as children, require parental consent and forbid behavioural tracking. Our stats count as tracking[19].
Licensed data onlyS&P 500 data on FRED can't be reproduced without S&P's permission. NSE end-of-day data is a paid subscription. Record a license for every dataset[20].
No tips or adviceScenarios teach process. No "buy X" signals. Keep the disclaimer visible.
Model vs fact labelsEvery chart says whether it's historical, synthetic or illustrative.
